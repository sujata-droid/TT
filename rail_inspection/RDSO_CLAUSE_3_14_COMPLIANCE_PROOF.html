1. Specification Requirement (Clause 3.14)

“3.14 Sampling distance/ measurement interval of the system should be user selectable of 0.25m or multiple of 0.25m and reporting/exporting interval can be decided by user. However, the sampling interval shall not be changed during measurement.”

5. On-Site Inspection Verification Procedure

  1. Sampling Resolution Selection: Tap CALIBRATE → Track Distance. Verify buttons for 0.25m, 0.50m, 0.75m, and 1.00m. Select 0.25m.
  2. Active Run Lockout: Tap START to begin measuring. Observe that CALIBRATE is disabled. The operator cannot modify the interval while running.
  3. Stationary Pause Check: Push the trolley 1 meter, then halt for 15 seconds. Observe that the point counter stops incrementing and no duplicate rows appear.
  4. Reporting Export Check: Tap STOP, insert USB drive, tap CSV / USB. Select 0.50m or 1.00m reporting interval. Export and verify clean decimation on the USB flash drive.
